A digital guidebook is a mobile-friendly resource providing vacation rental guests with check-in instructions, house manuals, Wi-Fi details, local recommendations, and checkout steps accessible via web browser before and during their stay. For hosts, digital guidebooks reduce repetitive messaging, enable self-check-in, and create upsell opportunities, while guests benefit from convenience, independence, and centralized information.
