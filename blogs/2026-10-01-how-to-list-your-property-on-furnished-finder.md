Quick Summary Listing on Furnished Finder takes about 20 minutes. You create an account, pay a $199 flat annual fee (no booking commission), complete your profile, fill in property details, set a monthly rate, and upload photos. The platform targets 30-plus-day stays for traveling professionals, primarily travel nurses.
